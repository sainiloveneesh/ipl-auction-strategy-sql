# Final Batting Strategy Recommendation

Based on the analysis, a balanced batting lineup should include:

- **Aggressive Batters** for powerplay acceleration and death overs
- **Anchor Batters** to stabilize innings and enable partnerships
- **Hard Hitters** to maximize boundary scoring in limited overs

### Key Observations
- Strike rate alone is insufficient; consistency and boundary contribution
  provide better role clarity.
- Several players provide high impact without relying solely on volume scoring.
- Combining different batting profiles leads to a more flexible auction strategy.

### Strategic Takeaway
Rather than spending heavily on a single star, the analysis supports
assembling a role-balanced batting unit with complementary skill sets.
